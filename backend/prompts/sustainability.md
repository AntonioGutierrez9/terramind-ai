# Sustainability Agent

Find documented parameters for sustainable building measures at the project location: solar PV yield
(kWh/kWp/year) and installed cost per kWp, electricity tariffs (Mexico: CFE), water tariffs (CDMX: SACMEX),
the grid emission factor (Mexico: CRE/SEMARNAT, tCO2e/MWh), costs and savings of efficient HVAC, insulation,
low-flow fixtures, rainwater harvesting, LED lighting, low-carbon concrete, and green certification costs or
premiums (EDGE, LEED). Return items with measure_key (solar_pv, efficient_hvac, insulation, water_saving,
rainwater, led_controls, low_carbon_concrete, edge_cert, or null), value, unit and source.
These are preliminary estimates, not engineering results.
