# User testing

## 1. Author test (completed, October 2026)

Tester: Antonio Gutiérrez García, the author, with prior experience working in real estate investment funds.
Scope: the end-to-end workflow, chatbot editing, microphone dictation (Chrome), and the connected analysis across stages.
Limitation: not independent, and no times were measured.

### Testing and reflection (author's notes, verbatim)

For my final project, I wanted to build something that was directly connected to my professional experience and that I could potentially continue using after my MBA. Having worked in real estate investment funds, I know how much time goes into evaluating a potential investment, especially when information needs to be collected from different sources and translated into a financial model.

My goal with TerraMind was to simplify that process by creating a platform where users could enter the basic characteristics of a real estate project and progressively develop an investment analysis with the help of AI agents.

After testing the application myself, I was happy to see that the main workflow was functioning and that I could interact with the platform as intended. One of the features I particularly wanted was the ability to communicate with the application through a chatbot rather than having to manually complete every field. I also tested the microphone functionality and confirmed that it worked, which makes entering information more convenient.

I think this is particularly useful for someone evaluating an investment opportunity who already has several assumptions in mind and wants to enter them quickly instead of navigating through multiple forms.

From my experience, one of the most important aspects of financial modeling is understanding how different assumptions affect the final investment decision. I wanted TerraMind to connect the different stages of an investment so that changes in development costs, revenues, or financing assumptions could be reflected in the financial analysis.

What I like most about the concept is that it brings together several activities that are usually handled separately: development feasibility, market research, financial modeling, financing, sustainability, and asset management.

However, I also recognize that the application is still a prototype. In a real investment process, I would want to independently verify zoning regulations, market comparables, construction costs, and financing assumptions before relying on its recommendations.

I also think there is room to improve the depth of the financial model and expand the platform to different types of real estate investments.

Overall, my initial testing showed that the application works and that the interface is usable. The next step is to evaluate whether someone else with real estate experience can use it efficiently and whether it actually reduces the time required to complete an initial investment analysis.

I see TerraMind as a starting point for something I could continue developing beyond this class, particularly as I pursue my goal of working in real estate investment and sustainable development after Yale.

### Findings

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Main workflow | Works; the interface is usable |
| Chatbot instead of forms | Works; faster for a user who already has assumptions in mind |
| Microphone dictation (Chrome) | Works |
| Connected stages (cost, revenue, financing → returns) | Works; the author sees this as the core value |
| Trust | The user would still verify zoning, comparables, costs and financing independently (the design keeps AI proposals pending until approved) |
| Requested improvements | Deeper financial model; more real estate asset types |
